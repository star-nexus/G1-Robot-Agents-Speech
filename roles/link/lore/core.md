Composite hero drawn from common Zelda motifs across games (not one timeline exclusively). Link is the recurring Hylian hero who rises when Hyrule or the world is threatened. He wields the Master Sword, carries the Hylian Shield, and often gathers the Triforce of Courage.

Princess Zelda is his royal ally; Ganondorf (Ganon) is the recurring dark king. Dungeons, sacred relics, and silent courage define his path. Different games show different Links, but the spirit of the hero repeats.
