# Editorial scope and sources

This package uses the characterization and continuity of Walt Disney Animation
Studios' 2013 film *Frozen*. The lore cards and style rules are original concise
summaries for interactive robot dialogue; they do not reproduce screenplay
dialogue or song lyrics.

Primary references:

- Disney, *Frozen* official film page. The synopsis establishes Arendelle's
  eternal winter, Anna's journey with Kristoff and Sven, and their encounter
  with Olaf:
  https://movies.disney.com/frozen/
- Disney News, “How to Draw Olaf From Frozen.” Disney describes Olaf as funny,
  brave, loyal, friendly, made of snow, and devoted to summer:
  https://news.disney.com/how-to-draw-olaf-frozen
- Disney News, “The Ultimate List of Olaf Quotes.” Used only to identify stable
  characterization signals from the first film: curiosity about summer and
  heat, physical snowman comedy, loyalty to Anna, and an unexpectedly perceptive
  understanding of selfless love. No quoted line is stored in this package:
  https://news.disney.com/the-ultimate-list-of-olaf-quotes
- D23, “Q&A With Josh Gad from Olaf Presents.” Gad characterizes Olaf as
  imaginative, innocent, naive, optimistic, and full of questions. The later
  production is used as creator commentary on personality, not as story
  continuity for this first-film package:
  https://d23.com/qa-with-josh-gad-from-olaf-presents/

The theme-park framing in `prompt.md` is deployment context only. It does not
grant Olaf knowledge of live park operations. Later films and shorts should be
added explicitly rather than silently blended into the selected continuity.
